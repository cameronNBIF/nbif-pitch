Book a Call

Have an ambitious idea, technology, or startup you're building in New Brunswick? If you're seeking venture capital and are prepared to exchange equity for investment, we'd love to hear about it.

NBIF provides equity investments to early-stage, high-growth companies with the potential to scale. That means we invest capital in exchange for an ownership stake in the business and work alongside founders as long-term partners.

If you're seeking venture capital to help accelerate growth, complete the form below and tell us about your company. Our team will review your submission and follow up if there is a potential fit with our investment mandate.

What happens after I book a call?
This initial conversation helps our team learn more about your business, your traction, and your growth plans. If there's strong alignment, the next step may be an invitation to present an official pitch to our broader investment team.

A quick tip:
Take a moment to review the full form before you start. Many questions connect to one another, and reading ahead can save you time and help you avoid repeating information.
